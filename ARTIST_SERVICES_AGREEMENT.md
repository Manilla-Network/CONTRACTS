## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"360° Agreement"** means this Artist Services Agreement covering distribution, marketing, analytics, and platform services.

1.2 **"Content"** means all music recordings, artwork, metadata, videos, and associated materials submitted by the Artist to the Platform.

1.3 **"Platform"** means the Manilla Network web application, API, distribution pipeline, and associated services at manilla.rald.cloud.

1.4 **"Distribution"** means the delivery of Content to Digital Service Providers (DSPs) including but not limited to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Audiomack, Boomplay, and others.

1.5 **"DSP"** means Digital Service Provider — any streaming or download platform that hosts distributed music.

1.6 **"Revenue"** means all monies earned from streams, downloads, sync licensing, and other exploitation of Content via the Platform.

1.7 **"ISRC"** means International Standard Recording Code assigned to each recording.

1.8 **"UPC"** means Universal Product Code assigned to each release.

## 2. GRANT OF RIGHTS

2.1 The Artist grants Manilla a **non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-bearing** license to:
  - Distribute Content to DSPs on the Artist's behalf
  - Encode, transcode, and format Content for DSP requirements
  - Create and manage FanLinks and promotional pages
  - Collect and remit royalties from DSPs
  - Provide analytics data to the Artist via the Platform

2.2 This license is granted for the **term of this Agreement** and terminates upon proper written termination by either party.

2.3 The Artist retains **full ownership** of all copyrights in the Content.

2.4 Manilla does **not** acquire publishing rights, sync rights, or any rights beyond those necessary to provide the Services.

## 3. DISTRIBUTION TERMS

3.1 **Admin-Controlled Distribution.** All distribution submissions are controlled exclusively by Manilla administrators. Artists may not initiate distribution directly. The process is:
  - Artist submits release for review
  - Admin reviews and approves
  - Admin initiates distribution to DSPs
  - Artist is notified upon successful distribution

3.2 **DDEX Standard.** All distributions use DDEX ERN 4.3 standard via Once.app.

3.3 **Distribution Timeline.** Manilla will use commercially reasonable efforts to distribute approved Content within 5–10 business days of admin approval.

3.4 **Takedowns.** Artist may request Content removal from DSPs with 30 days written notice. Manilla will process takedown requests within 15 business days.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 **Term:** This Agreement commences on the date of countersignature and continues for **12 months**, automatically renewing for successive 12-month terms unless terminated.

7.2 **Termination by Artist:** Artist may terminate with **30 days written notice** to contracts@rald.cloud.

7.3 **Termination by Manilla:** Manilla may terminate immediately for:
  - Material breach of Section 5 (Artist Obligations)
  - Content infringing third-party intellectual property
  - Fraudulent activity on the Platform

7.4 **Effect of Termination:**
  - Manilla will initiate DSP takedowns within 15 business days
  - Outstanding royalties will be paid within 30 days post-termination
  - Platform access will be revoked on termination date
