# Manilla Network — Revenue Share Guide

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-06-01

---

## Overview

Manilla collects streaming royalties from DSPs and remits them to artists monthly. This document explains how revenue flows from DSP to artist.

---

## Revenue Flow

```
DSP (Spotify, Apple Music, Audiomack, etc.)
    │
    │ Monthly royalty statement
    ▼
Once.app (distribution & collection partner)
    │
    │ Net payment (after Once.app fees)
    ▼
Manilla Network
    │
    │ Platform service fee deducted
    ▼
Artist Wallet (Manilla Dashboard)
    │
    │ Monthly payout (when threshold met)
    ▼
Artist Bank Account (via SquadCo)
```

---

## Revenue Split by Plan

| Plan | Artist Share | Manilla Fee | Notes |
|------|-------------|------------|-------|
| FREE | 70% | 30% | Up to 1 release/month |
| STARTER | 75% | 25% | Up to 3 releases/month |
| PRO | 85% | 15% | Unlimited releases |
| SUPERSTAR | 90% | 10% | Unlimited + priority review |
| CONTRACT (360°) | Negotiated | Negotiated | Per individual agreement |
| PAY_PER_TRACK | 80% | 20% | Per-release purchase |
| PAY_PER_ALBUM | 82% | 18% | Per-album purchase |

*Revenue splits are subject to change with 30 days notice.*

---

## Payment Schedule

| Event | Timeline |
|-------|---------|
| DSP reports streams | ~45–60 days after the stream month |
| Once.app remits to Manilla | Net 30 after DSP payment |
| Manilla pays artist | Net 30 after receipt (monthly cycle) |
| Total artist payment lag | ~75–90 days from stream date |

**Example:**
- January streams → DSP reports ~March → Manilla receives ~April → Artist paid ~May

---

## Minimum Payout Threshold

₦5,000 NGN per payout cycle. Balances below this threshold roll over to the next month.

---

## Royalty Sources

| Source | Included |
|--------|---------|
| Streaming (Spotify, Apple Music, etc.) | ✅ |
| Download sales | ✅ |
| YouTube Content ID | ✅ (where configured) |
| Sync licensing | ✅ (when facilitated by Manilla) |
| Performance royalties (COSON) | ❌ (collect directly from COSON) |
| Mechanical royalties | ❌ (collect directly from your PRO) |

- Monthly breakdown
- Per-release breakdown
- Per-DSP breakdown
- Pending payouts
- Payment history

---

## Disputes

If you believe your royalty statement is inaccurate:
1. Email royalties@rald.cloud with your artist ID and the specific discrepancy
2. Manilla will investigate within 10 business days
3. If confirmed, adjustment is made in the next payout cycle

---

## Tax

Artists are responsible for their own tax obligations. Manilla does not withhold tax. At year-end, Manilla provides a royalty statement summary for your records.

---

## Currency

All payouts are in Nigerian Naira (NGN) via SquadCo unless a separate arrangement is made in writing.
